Real estate · WhatsApp lead generation01 / Suvini Group – Kulswamini Homes

Local property enquiries and site visits

Two campaigns for the same real estate client, targeting local buyers around Kalyan, Bhiwandi, Dombivli, Ambernath and nearby areas.

Combined ad spend₹24,762.18
Messaging conversations233
Weighted cost / conversation₹106.28
Verified site visits30
Campaign breakdown
CampaignConversationsSpendCost/result
Kulswamini Traffic133₹15,214.89₹114.40
Rutu Traffic100₹9,547.29₹95.47
Strategy & execution
  • Local geographic targeting across Kalyan-region catchments, with selected state exclusions.
  • Housing special ad category was selected in the Kulswamini ad set.
  • Age range 25–65+ and all genders; real-estate interest targeting included apartment, condominium and first-time-buyer interests.
  • WhatsApp messaging objective, property-led offers and seasonal Diwali creative replacement.
  • Compared image and reel ad performance: one image ad generated 79 conversations at ₹82.06; one reel generated 39 at ₹123.66.
Business outcome & learnings
  • 30 property site visits were reported for Kulswamini. Based on 133 conversations, the indicative conversation-to-visit rate is 22.56% and cost per visit is ₹507.16.
  • Rutu's downstream site visits and sales were not available.
  • Image creative had a lower cost per conversation than the reported reel during the measured period; this is a performance comparison, not a controlled A/B test.
  • Audience breakdowns showed more messaging conversations from men in the displayed reports; lead quality by gender was not available.
The 30 site visits are attributed to Kulswamini only, not to the combined 233 conversations. No property bookings or sales are claimed.
